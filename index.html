How it works

Three steps to numbers you trust

Confidence comes from knowing exactly how far each swing carries. WedgeWise turns your own shots into numbers you can count on.

STEP 1

Log your shots

On the course, pace off from the flag to the pitch mark. On a launch monitor, type each carry. Either way takes seconds.

STEP 2

Your numbers turn green

Every club and swing gets its own number from your shots. Five shots in and it turns green: a number you can trust.

STEP 3

See today’s numbers

Enter today’s temperature and elevation, or tap for your location, and every number moves to what it carries right now.

The adjustment, in the open

No black box

About 1% of carry per 10°F and 1.1% per 1,000 ft of elevation, from published launch monitor research.

today = baseline

  × (1 + 0.011 · elevation / 1000)

Questions

When can I get WedgeWise?

WedgeWise is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone. Follow along at wedgewise.app for the launch.

Is it free?

The free version covers your lob, sand and gap wedges with the ½ · ¾ · Full system, both entry types, and your baseline numbers. WedgeWise Pro ($6.99 a month or $49.99 a year) adds the pitching wedge and 9 iron, today’s adjusted numbers, a custom swing system, and Swing Check.

Do I need a launch monitor?

No. On-Course Entry works with just a rangefinder or the yardage on a sprinkler head: distance to the flag, and the paces from the flag to the pitch mark.

How accurate is the temperature and elevation adjustment?

It uses published launch monitor research: about 1% of carry per 10°F and 1.1% per 1,000 ft. Because every shot is normalized the same way, your numbers stay fair whether you practiced on a cold morning or a hot afternoon. More on the Support page.
