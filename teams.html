Questions coaches ask

What do my players need?

An iPhone with WedgeWise installed. They sign in, enter your team code in Settings, and their Pro features switch on right away.

Can we pay with a purchase order?

Yes. Ask for an invoice and we’ll switch your team on as soon as it’s paid.

Do coaches get the app too?

Yes. The head coach and one assistant coach each get a free WedgeWise Pro account with your coach code. Coach accounts don’t use any of your 12 player spots.

What if we have more than 12 players?

Add as many as you need for $39 a year each. Email us and we’ll add the spots.

What happens when a player leaves the team?

Their spot opens up for someone else. The player keeps their shots and numbers in their own account.
